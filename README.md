**Smoking Behavior in Special Regency of Yogyakarta using Logistic Regression**

**Description** 
<br> Smoking is an inseparable part of people’s daily lives. Currently, smoking has become the world’s biggest health problem. 
Cigarettes contain substances that are harmful to the body, contributing to the worsening of a person’s health condition.
According to the Indonesian Central Bureau of Statistics, cigarettes rank second highest in household per capita expenditure after rice. 
People prefer to spend money on cigarettes rather than buying something more useful for themselves. 
Smoking is associated with people with low socioeconomic status. 
Socioeconomic status is determined by several indicators, namely education, occupation, income, and expenditure. 
Therefore, this analysis contains information about the influence of individual sociodemographic and socioeconomic characteristics on smoking behavior.
<br> This analysis’s helps to understand smokers characteristics in Special Regency of Yogyakarta. 
The results of this analysis help the government make framework regulations to reduce smoking behavior. 
This analysis may identify some high-risk group of individuals which could lead to policy-making target these specific groups.

**Data**
<br> The analysis uses secondary data from the Indonesia Family Life Survey (IFLS) 5, sourced from RAND (https://www.rand.org/well-being/social-and-behavioral-policy/data/FLS/IFLS/ifls5.html), with a sample of 610 people from the Special Region of Yogyakarta. 
The data used pertains to residents aged 15 years and above. 
The variables used in this study are smoking status as the dependent variable, and gender, age, education, occupation, marital status, residence, and household expenditure as independent variables.
